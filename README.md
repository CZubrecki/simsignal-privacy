SimSignal Inc. ("SimSignal", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the SimSignal mobile application ("App") and our services.

By using SimSignal, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use our App or services.

**1. Information We Collect**

1.1 Personal Information
We collect the following personal information from you:

Email Address
• Your email address is the only personal data we collect and store.
• We collect your email address when you create an account.
• Your email address is used for authentication, communication, and account management.

1.2 Authentication Data

One-Time Passwords (OTP)
• We use Supabase for authentication services.
• When you log in, we send a one-time password to your email address via Resend.
• OTP codes are temporary and expire after a short period.
• We do not store your passwords as we use passwordless authentication.

1.3 Payment Information

Payment Processing
• All payment information is collected and processed by Stripe, our third-party payment processor.
• We do NOT collect, store, or have access to your credit card numbers, banking information, or other payment credentials.
• Stripe may share transaction details with us (such as transaction ID, amount, and status) but does not share your full payment card details.
• Your payment information is subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.

1.4 eSIM Service Data

eSIM Activation and Usage
• When you purchase and activate an eSIM, certain technical information is processed by esimAccess, our eSIM connectivity provider.
• This may include device identifiers (IMEI, EID) necessary for eSIM provisioning and activation.
• Usage data (data consumption, connection logs) may be collected by esimAccess and local network carriers.
• We receive limited service information from esimAccess to manage your account and provide customer support.

1.5 Automatically Collected Information

Usage Data
We may automatically collect certain information about your device and how you interact with our App, including:
• Device type and model
• Operating system version (iOS version)
• App version
• Unique device identifiers
• IP address
• App usage statistics and analytics
• Crash reports and error logs

Cookies and Tracking Technologies
• We may use cookies, beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect information and improve our services.
• You can control cookie settings through your device settings, though this may affect App functionality.
